# Demo media attribution

All files come from Wikimedia Commons and were retrieved on 2026-10-27. Only CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA and public-domain files were accepted.
Photos: originals wider than 1600 px were fetched as the Commons 1920 px thumbnail (the API now returns 1920 px for `iiurlwidth=1600`) and downscaled locally to 1600 px wide (Lanczos, JPEG q90); originals of 1600 px or less are unmodified. Videos are unmodified Commons files.
Licenses were read from the Commons file pages at retrieval time; where a row says `flickr-import-bot-unreviewed` the license was copied from Flickr by a bot and was not reviewed by a Commons volunteer.
**CC BY / CC BY-SA require attribution (author, license, link) wherever the media is shown. CC BY-SA also requires derivatives (crops, overlays) be shared under the same license.**
Some photos show identifiable people; 1 file carries a Commons "personality rights" warning (noted below). Do not present them as anything other than flood documentation.

## Notes and gaps

- The Commons categories requested ("2020 Central Vietnam floods", "Floods in Huế", "Floods in Hội An", "Floods in Da Nang", "Floods in Ho Chi Minh City") either do not exist or are empty; photos were found through "Floods in Vietnam" (and subcategories), "2020 floods in Vietnam", "Videos of floods in Vietnam", and keyword searches.
- Central Vietnam coverage: Quảng Ngãi (Nov 2013, with GPS), Huế (2020, boat only) and Quảng Trị (boat only). Street-level photos of 2020 central Vietnam floods with scooters/people were not found under a reusable license.
- Other usable Commons videos NOT downloaded (limit of 2): `Inundita strato en Quang Ngai.webm` (CC BY-SA 4.0, 19 s, 14.4 MB, static view of a flooded street, no vehicles), `Vadado laŭ inundita strato en Quang Ngai 4.webm` (CC BY-SA 4.0, 23 s, 7.4 MB, scooter traffic and crowd at the water edge), `Vadado laŭ inundita strato en Quang Ngai.webm` (CC BY-SA 4.0, 38 s, 26.1 MB), `Hội An sơ tán khách du lịch vì bão Damrey.webm` (public domain per Commons, 60 s, 23.6 MB; its Commons description text does not match the title, so the content was not verified). No mp4 flood videos of Vietnam were found; all candidates are webm/ogv. All are in Commons `Category:Videos of floods in Vietnam` or `Category:Videos of aftermath of Typhoon Haiyan in Quang Ngai`.
- The two videos have no coordinates on their Commons pages; photos by the same author the same day are at about 15.09 N, 108.82 E (Quảng Ngãi city).
- Haithanh's Hanoi 2008 photos (CC BY 2.0) were imported from Flickr by a bot and carry no FlickreviewR tag; confirm the Flickr license is still CC BY before any public release. The 2008 Hanoi flood was in Oct-Nov 2008; the Commons EXIF times are camera clock values.
- Water-level wording in the table is a rough visual estimate by the curator, not a measurement.
